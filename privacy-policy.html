Privacy Policy — HOTSPACE VPN

Last updated: August 2026

HOTSPACE VPN ("the App") is a connection client for SSH and SlowDNS (DNS tunnel) servers. This policy explains what information the App accesses, why, and how it is handled.

1. What the App is — and isn't

HOTSPACE VPN does not operate, own, or provide its own VPN servers. It is a tool for connecting to server configurations you import or that are provided to you by a third party (for example, an SSH/SlowDNS server you or someone else controls). We do not operate the servers your traffic is routed through, and we do not see or store the content of your internet traffic.

2. Information the App accesses

  • Server connection details you enter — hostnames, usernames, and passwords for SSH/SlowDNS servers you choose to connect to. These are stored locally on your device to let you reconnect without re-entering them, and are sent only to the server you're connecting to (never to us).
  • Connection/network statistics — upload and download data usage, connection status, and basic device/network info (e.g. Android version, network type) shown to you in the app's Logs screen. This is used only to display stats to you and to help diagnose connection issues; it is not sold or shared.
  • Device identifiers for ads — the App shows optional ads (used to grant extra connection time) through Google's advertising SDK (Google AdMob / Play Services Ads). Google may collect an advertising ID and related data under its own privacy policy to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not log or monitor the content of your browsing activity.
  • We do not share your server credentials with anyone other than the server you connect to.

4. Data storage

Server profiles and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this locally stored data.

5. Third-party services

The App uses Google Play Services and Google's advertising SDK to show ads. These third-party services may collect data as described in their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for their data practices.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
